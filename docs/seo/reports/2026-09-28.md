## What I fixed
Two posts published by the Competitor Radar this week showed up on the crawl issue list: their
titles ran long and their meta/OG/Twitter descriptions ran over 160 characters.
`lawn-care-marketing-agency-recurring-revenue` and `forestry-mulching-marketing` both got a
seoTitle (57 and 54 characters) added to `_blog.json`, and their meta, OG, and Twitter
descriptions were trimmed to under 145 characters directly in their HTML (individual post
descriptions are not generator-driven, only the `<title>`/OG/Twitter title tags are). Fixing the
title tags via `seoTitle` also cleared up a pre-existing double-HTML-escaping bug in both posts'
og:title and twitter:title (`&amp;amp;` rendering as literal text instead of `&`).

Ran the full mechanical-fix sweep: broken internal links, missing seoTitles, meta descriptions
over 158 characters, missing article-cta-link lines, blog posts missing from the sitemap, and
FAQ schema parsing. Everything else came back clean. Ran the full build and check pipeline
(gen_blog, gen_blog_schema, build, build --check, check_links, check_structured_data,
check_blog_images); all pass.

Two posts (land-clearing-marketing, best-green-industry-marketing-agencies) are still missing
their CTA block entirely, not just the link line inside it. That is new copy, not a mechanical
fix, so I left them again this week.

No open seo/ pull requests to merge this week.
